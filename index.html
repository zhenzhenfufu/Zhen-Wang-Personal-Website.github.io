About

I am a second-year undergraduate at UC San Diego in Data Science. I build AI agents and machine learning systems, and I care most about the part that usually gets skipped: showing that the output is actually correct. This summer I spent three months at Pfizer China R&D building an agent-assisted pipeline that turns clinical trial data into regulator-ready filings — a place where an unverified answer is not an answer.

I work in the Rare AI Lab (Prof. Aobo Li, HDSI & Physics) on machine learning for physics time series, and I am always glad to talk about agent evaluation — zhw124@ucsd.edu.

What the course agent actually sees

Every course the Data Science major requires, and what each one waits on — parsed by my planning agent out of catalog sentences written for humans, not for parsers. Hover or tab to a course to light up everything it depends on. That closure is the part the model is not allowed to get wrong: asked for a whole multi-quarter plan in one shot it was legal 40% of the time; generating one quarter at a time against a recomputed eligible set, 100% across 20 tasks.
