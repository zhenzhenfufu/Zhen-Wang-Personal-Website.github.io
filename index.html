What I am working on now

SIDERIUS

An open-source auto-ML agent for science · Rare AI Lab, UC San Diego

Give it a dataset and a research question. It proposes models, writes them, trains and evaluates them, and uses what came back to decide what to try next — keeping a record of every iteration, and checking validity alongside the score so a model that wins for the wrong reason gets caught. It runs as a fixed workflow, or with an LLM orchestrator that composes the research steps itself.

  • Orchestration — running and tuning it
  • End-to-end testing on Project 8 physics data
  • Physics guidance for the agent
  • Defect reports upstream

It is open source and we want it used. If you have a dataset and a question, try it — and tell us what broke.

What the course agent actually sees

Every course the Data Science major requires, and what each one waits on — parsed by my planning agent out of catalog sentences written for humans, not for parsers. Hover or tab to a course to light up everything it depends on. That closure is the part the model is not allowed to get wrong: asked for a whole multi-quarter plan in one shot it was legal 40% of the time; generating one quarter at a time against a recomputed eligible set, 100% across 20 tasks.
