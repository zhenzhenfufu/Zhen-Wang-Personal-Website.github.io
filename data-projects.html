Data Science & Modeling

Four projects, in the order they taught me something: a year of real research data, a model I had to derive rather than fit, a dataset large enough to be dishonest with, and a question I wanted the answer to for its own sake.

Shanghai Jiao Tong University — Research Assistant

Jun 2024 – Sep 2025 · pre-college, remote · Prof. Xiaoning Sun ↗

  • Automated the processing of 500+ hours of child behavioural recordings into 95 standardised reports with Stata and a structured pipeline, replacing per-session manual work.
  • Designed survey instruments covering 60 socioeconomic and family-environment variables, and independently administered assessments for 28 children (Wechsler Scales, TEC).

Wound Healing Geometry Simulator

Spring 2026 · Mathematical modelling · GitHub ↗

  • Simulated wound closure across three geometric dimensions — width, length, depth — by explicit Euler integration of a coupled system.
  • Encoded the biology as latent state driving visible metrics: fibrin clot, macrophage activity, TGF-β signalling, fibroblast activity, collagen maturity.
  • Produced dual-panel animations (top-view ellipse shrinkage, side-view depth reduction) and CSV time-series exports with NumPy, Matplotlib and Pandas.

Steam Commercial Success Prediction

Spring 2026 · Project lead · COGS 108

  • Led a team through cleaning, exploratory analysis and regression on 27,000+ Steam titles to quantify how pricing strategy and pre-release tags relate to revenue.
  • Most of the work was in the cleaning: at this size, the convenient join and the correct join give different answers.

Premier League Transfer Efficiency

Jan – Mar 2026 · Personal project

  • Scraped ten years of transfer spending and goal difference for all 20 clubs, then tested whether spend predicts results, with linear regression and hypothesis testing.
