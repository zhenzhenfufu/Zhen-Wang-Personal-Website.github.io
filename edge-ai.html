Thermal Throttling & the Energy Cost of Inference

Independent research · Advised by Prof. Peter Chi

Benchmarks for edge inference are usually reported on a cold device. The question I wanted to answer is what happens on the tenth minute: how much throughput a Jetson actually keeps once it is hot, and what that costs in energy per token.

Setup

  • Llama-3 workloads on NVIDIA Jetson, benchmarked automatically across the board's power modes.
  • GPU temperature, clock frequency, power draw and throughput (tokens/sec) logged through nvidia-smi and Prometheus, so throttling shows up as a time series rather than a single average.

Honest status

This work is still early. The instrumentation runs and the data is accumulating; I have no published result, and the design is my first self-authored experiment rather than a settled methodology. It is on this site because it is the project where I am learning how to ask a measurable question, not because it has an answer yet.
